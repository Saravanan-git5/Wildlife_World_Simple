Welcome to Wildlife World

Wildlife means all the animals, birds and plants that live in nature. Forests are important homes for many living things.

Wildlife keeps nature balanced. We must protect forests, rivers and animals for the future.

Why is wildlife important?

  • It keeps nature balanced.
  • It supports the food chain.
  • It makes our environment healthy.
